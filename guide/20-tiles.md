# Part III · The tiles {#part-tiles}

One section per tile, grouped by tab and in the order the tab shows them. Each starts
with a fact box filled in from the dashboard's own records: the source and its licence,
how often the series is published and how long after the period it covers, its unit and
indicator class, who computes it, and what the tile draws. Then:

- **What it is**: the concept and why it matters for the cycle.
- **Computation**: how the number is calculated, by its publisher or by the dashboard.
- **Reading it**: the badge's exact rules and what levels and changes mean.
- **Use in practice**: lead times, typical signals and how it combines with other tiles.
- **Caveats**: revisions, breaks, coverage and known false signals.
